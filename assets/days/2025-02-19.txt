This is a "Future Enterprise". Back in '95 (ugh! 30 years ago!) this was the rarest card in Star Trek CCG, a collectible card game (now defunct) by Decipher Inc. 
My teen self really wanted this card back then, but it was out of my league. 
Fast forward 20-something years later, I was able to get it on eBay for around 20€. Oh well, it still feels magical.
